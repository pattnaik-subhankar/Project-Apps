Cyclone Scenario Predictor

Paste the latest IMD or JTWC cyclone advisory. The module turns it into a 3-scenario outlook (most likely, stronger & faster, weaker & slower), district-level wind impact tiers, and a T-minus preparation countdown.

THE SCIENCE: Nature (2026) reports WeatherNext Cyclones, the Google DeepMind + NOAA operational AI model: ensemble forecasts of cyclone track, intensity and size up to 15 days out, with an average of a day or more of lead-time advantage over leading operational models, and accuracy gains comparable to a decade of operational development. Large ensembles capture rare events better. This page applies that mindset, the lead-time advantage and the tail-risk framing on top of official bulletins. It is a readiness layer, not a forecast model.

5 · Prep countdown

Lead time is the paper's headline result. Here it becomes a checklist. Times are estimates from the advisory's landfall window.
How to read this: this module parses official bulletins and applies planning logic. It does not run WN-C or any AI forecast model. Wind radii are climatological planning defaults, not model output. IMD, JTWC, NDMA and OSDMA warnings remain authoritative. When in doubt, follow the official order to evacuate.
